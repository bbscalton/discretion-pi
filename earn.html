Earn with Discretion

Solid fieldwork for adult relationship cases. You get paid for approved evidence — quality and lawful public observation first. Clients never see your name.

Split: platform 30% / investigator 70% of the client evidence fee. High-proof approved sets earn an extra bonus. Witness statements and monitoring assignments pay separately. Payouts release after client unlock and case-manager confirmation.

How you get paid

Earnings move in stages. Nothing pays out until quality review and client unlock clear — then your case manager confirms the transfer.

  1. Capture & tag Upload clear, lawful public observations with court-oriented tags.
  2. Approval Case team reviews quality. Approved items become payout-eligible.
  3. Client unlock When the client package unlocks, your earnings move to held, then paid.
  4. Transfer Case manager marks payouts paid — bank transfer arranged with you.

Standard — documentation

Earn GYD$31,500 (70% of GYD$45,000) for approved public togetherness: dining, walking, clear identity in a lawful public place.

  • Two adults clearly together in a public venue
  • Faces / ID clarity when identity is the point
  • Context — place, time, sequence — helps the Court Package
  • Adults 18+ only · public sidewalks and open venues only

Strong — kissing & affection

Earn GYD$66,500 (70% of GYD$95,000) when approved captures show kissing or clear public affection.

  • Catching them kissing in public view
  • Holding hands, arm-in-arm, hug with clear contact
  • Both parties identifiable when the case requires it
  • Tasteful, court-oriented frames — never graphic; lawful public observation only

High-proof — making out / passionate affection

Earn GYD$137,500 total when the set clears the premium bar: GYD$122,500 (70% of GYD$175,000) plus a GYD$15,000 high-proof bonus after approval. This means making out — passionate kissing and affection — not graphic imagery.

  • Documented in public places, in a vehicle (from a lawful public vantage — exterior / silhouette), or as part of hotel/house arrival patterns where observation is lawful
  • Clear passionate kissing / making out — court-oriented, tasteful frames
  • Never bedrooms, never trespass, never illegal devices, never nude or explicit content
  • Tag making_out, hotel entry, or overnight patterns when appropriate
  • Adults 18+ · case team decides if the set meets the bonus bar

High-proof — hotel / house arrival

Arrival patterns also support the high-proof bar when paired with strong affection evidence — still public-area observation only.

  • Entering a hotel lobby or house together from a public sidewalk
  • Arrive/leave sequences with timestamps for the Court Package
  • Public areas only — never inside rooms

Witness statements

Earn GYD$35,000 (70% of GYD$50,000) when an approved third-party witness confirms they saw the subjects together — written or recorded statement with date, place, and what they observed.

  • People confirming they saw them together in public
  • Discreet interview; notebook or signed statement where appropriate
  • Tag witness_statement · separate add-on from photo tiers
  • No confrontation · adults 18+ · voluntary cooperation only

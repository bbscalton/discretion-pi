Client guide

How to open Discretion Client, submit your case, send leads, follow Case team updates, and view approved evidence after unlock.

Adults only. Discretion handles adult consensual-relationship and infidelity matters only. We do not investigate minors or predators. You never see investigator identity — only “Case team.” Logins are created by your case manager — there is no public Create account. Learn & strength content is educational information — not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can diagnose.

1. Install & sign in

Install Companion Notes (client access). Your case manager creates the login — you only sign in.

  • Download the preview APK from the button above (or the latest GitHub Release).
  • Open the file and install. If prompted, allow install from browser / Files.
  • Sign in with the email and temporary password your case manager shared.
  • Do not use the Schedule Helper build — that is a separate app for the field team.

2. Submit intake / open a case

Start with a confidential brief: subject context, locations, and timeline. Your case manager reviews intake before assignment.

  • Confirm the matter involves adults only.
  • Be factual: who, where, when — avoid speculation.
  • Status begins at intake, then moves through assigned → active → under review → ready to unlock → unlocked as work progresses.
  • Website intake requests are a first contact; full case work continues in the Client app after your account is provisioned.

Packages & payment

Packages are priced in GYD. Evidence unlock fees apply when your case manager releases approved vault materials. Ask Case team which package fits — payment is arranged after unlock (invoice), not as public self-checkout.

3. Upload leads

Send photos and notes that help your case (messaging screenshots, location hints, timelines). Leads are reviewed and sanitized before any field release.

  • Upload clear screenshots and photos from your gallery.
  • Add short factual captions: date, place, why it matters.
  • Do not confront the subject or tip them off while collecting leads.
  • Investigator identity stays hidden; you will not see who works the case.

3b. Partner activity / incident log

On a case, open Activity to document dated facts: what happened, optional impact, optional educational pattern tags, and an optional photo. Entries sync to your secure case file in the cloud.

  • Document facts with dates — useful for personal records and potential court presentation of facts.
  • Entries are reviewed before field visibility or Court Package inclusion.
  • Pattern tags (e.g. love-bombing cycle) are educational labels — not diagnoses.
  • This log does not diagnose NPD or prove someone “is a narcissist.”

Also open Learn & strength from home for educational modules — or read the web version at learn.html.

4. Status updates & Case team chat

Follow your case timeline and message Case team — support chat, never investigator legal names.

  • Timeline moves from intake through assignment, active work, review, and unlock.
  • During active field work, updates stay high-level by design.
  • Ask Case team questions in-app; do not expect real-time field narration.
  • You will never see investigator identity in chat or status.

5. Evidence vault — locked until unlock

Approved field evidence stays in a locked vault until your case manager unlocks the package. Payment for unlock tiers is arranged then — see pricing.

  • While locked: you see status and Case team updates, not media.
  • Captures are reviewed before they can appear in your package.
  • After unlock: view the approved Court Package / evidence released for you.
  • Clients cannot unlock a case themselves.

6. What to look out for

  • Privacy — keep case talk private; do not post about an active investigation.
  • Do not confront the subject, third parties, or anyone you believe is involved.
  • High-level updates during field work — limited detail while the case is active is normal.
  • Evidence only after unlock — vault media appears when your case manager unlocks, not sooner.
  • Adults only — Discretion does not take minor or predator matters.
  • Identity hidden — if someone claims to be “your investigator,” contact Case team through the app.

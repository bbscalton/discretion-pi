How it works

One discreet pipeline from intake to unlock — investigator identity stays hidden.

  1. Intake Share subject context and leads for admin review. Adults-only matters only.
  2. Assignment Operations assigns field investigators; you only see “case team.”
  3. Field evidence Tagged captures go through admin approval before any client visibility.
  4. Unlock When the case is ready, admin unlocks the approved Court Package for you.

What we handle

Adult consensual-relationship and infidelity matters only. We do not investigate matters involving minors or predators.

  • Discreet surveillance uploads & notes (no live streaming in v1)
  • Court-oriented tagging: public togetherness, contact, lodging patterns, ID clarity
  • Chain-of-custody fields and admin review before unlock
